Flow Field Theremin

Thousands of particles drift through an invisible field of swirling noise. Move your mouse (or finger) across the canvas to bend the field and play a synthesizer — your horizontal position controls pitch, and your vertical position controls filter brightness.

  • Move / drag — steer the particle flow and play notes
  • Click / tap — burst of particles + a plucked note
  • Use the control panel (top-right) to reshape the system live

Audio requires a click to start (browser policy).
